How it Works

1

Input Methods

You can add up to 5 sources using different formats:

  • Username: bugracoskun → spins from their Watchlist.
  • Shorthand: official/letterboxds-top-500-films → loads a specific list.
  • Full URL: Paste any link like letterboxd.com/official/list/top-500... directly.
2

Field Detection

Each field is validated as you type:

  • Shows a Watchlist or List badge instantly next to the field.
  • Marks Invalid entries (wrong domains, incomplete paths) with a red indicator.
  • Displays the resolved full URL below the input for confirmation.
3

How Selection Works

When you hit spin:

  • A random page is picked from the list, then a random film from that page.
  • Works with lists of any size, including 5,000+ entries.
  • Film details (poster, rating, backdrop) are fetched separately.
4

Auto-Shorten

When you paste a full Letterboxd URL:

  • The URL is erased character by character with a typewriter effect.
  • Then the short form (e.g. bugracoskun/turkish) is typed back in.
  • The original full URL is preserved below the input as a reference.
5

Sharing & Mix

Create shareable links from your list combinations:

  • Share Link: Generates a unique URL containing all your current lists — recipients get an instant spin.
  • URL Format: ?url=bugracoskun/turkish,bugracoskun — supports up to 5 comma-separated sources.
  • Browse-only: Append &run=0 to load lists without auto-spinning.
6

History & Quick Access

Your lists and results are saved locally:

  • Recent Lists: Previously used lists appear below the form — click any to instantly load it.
  • Activity Log: Your last 4 spins are saved with timestamps, film names, and poster art.
  • One-Click Reuse: Tap a recent list to auto-fill it into the next available field.
7

Privacy & Security

All data is stored in your browser:

  • 100% Local: All history and mixes are stored in your browser's localStorage.
  • No Tracking: No analytics, no cookies, no server-side storage of any kind.
  • Open Source: The entire codebase is publicly available for inspection.
