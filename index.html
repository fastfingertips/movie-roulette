How it Works

1

Flexible Input Formats

You can mix up to 5 sources using any format:

  • Username: bugracoskun → spins from their Watchlist.
  • Shorthand: official/letterboxds-top-500-films → loads a specific list.
  • Full URL: Paste any full letterboxd.com link directly.
  • Watchlist vs List: Distinguishes standard watchlists from custom lists named watchlist.
2

Live Badges & Ratio Borders

Each field is inspected and enriched as you type:

  • Shows real-time Watchlist or List badges with live film counts.
  • Displays dynamic odds percentages and animated ratio border bars.
  • Surfaces detailed error tooltips if a user or list is private or not found.
  • Automatically catches and warns about duplicate list entries.
3

Dual Random & Sampling Modes

Control your selection algorithm and list odds:

  • Deep Sample (Default): Multi-page uniform sampling across the entire catalog depth. Fast, reliable, and Cloudflare-safe.
  • Use /shuffle (Toggle): Opt-in to Letterboxd's native /by/shuffle view (samples from Page 1).
  • Equal Odds (Scale Toggle): Each list gets an equal 1/N chance of being selected first when combining multiple lists.
4

Auto-Shorten & Sync

Pasted full URLs are neatly formatted:

  • Long URLs are automatically shortened using a smooth typewriter animation.
  • The canonical URL is preserved below the input for reference.
  • Your active mix automatically syncs to the address bar for easy bookmarking.
5

Mixes & Share Links

Combine favorite lists to create custom cinematic blends:

  • Combine Sources: Add up to 5 lists to draw random picks across multiple tastes simultaneously.
  • Share Mix: Click Share Mix to copy a shareable blend link to your clipboard.
  • URL Parameter: Instant sharing with clean parameter syntax: ?url=official/letterboxds-top-500-films,...
6

Recent Lists & History

Your activity is persisted locally in your browser:

  • Recent Lists: Quick-access chips show inclusion status (#1, #2) — click to add or remove.
  • Activity Log: Saves your last 4 spins with timestamps, list sources, and Letterboxd links.
  • Keyboard Support: Navigate recent chips with Tab and toggle with Enter or Space.
7

Technical Logs & Waterfall

Inspect exact diagnostics for every spin:

  • Click the info icon on the result card to view technical logs.
  • Review exact probability percentages, pool breakdown, and winner verification.
  • Inspect pipeline execution waterfall timings (Metadata → Selection → Film Enrichment).
8

Privacy & 7 Custom Themes

Fast, lightweight, and customizable:

  • 100% Local Storage: Zero tracking, zero third-party analytics, and no server cookies.
  • 7 Curated Themes: Switch between Classic Light/Dark, Pine & Orchid, Citron & Berry, Forest & Lime, AMOLED Black, and Midnight Lime.
  • Open Source: Transparent and publicly hosted on GitHub.
