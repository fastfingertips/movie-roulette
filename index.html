How it Works

1

Input Lists

Paste a Letterboxd list URL or just type a username to use their Watchlist. You can combine up to 5 sources. Press Shift + Enter to quickly add more fields.

2

Smart Selection

Our optimized engine picks a random page and film instantly. This keeps things lightning-fast, even for lists with 5,000+ movies.

3

Quick Shortcuts

Bypass the UI by adding ?url=username or ?url=user/list/slug to the address bar for instant spins.

4

Local History

Your recent choices are saved locally on your device. We never store your data on our servers.
